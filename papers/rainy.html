Idealized Moist Convection

Vallis, G. K., Parker, D. and Tobias, S. 2018. A Simple System for Moist Convection. The Rainy-Benard Model. J. Fluid Mech. Submitted.

Click to download PDF file (4 MB)

This paper presents and explores the properties of an idealized model of moist convection, one that that makes connections to classical Rayleigh-Benard model and allows moist convection to be explored at a fundamental level.
