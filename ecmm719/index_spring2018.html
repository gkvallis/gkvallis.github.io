Introduction

This is a web site for material related to the module Fluid Dynamics of the Atmosphere and Ocean (ECMM719). It may be used in conjunction with the ELE site, which is only available to University of Exeter students.

  • Credit value: 15
  • Pre-requisites: Undergraduate maths, physics or engineering, including vector calculus and fluid dynamics. Ideally classes equivalent to ECM2706 and ECM3707. Or permission of the instructor.
  • Assessment:
    • Written exam (closed book) 80%
    • Course work. 20%. 2 assessed problem sets, various unassessed sets.
  • Lectures times:
    • Tuesday 9.30am, Harrison 203
    • Thu 1.30 pm, Harrison 203
    • Fri 12.30 pm, Harrison 203

  • Office Hours:
    • Tuesday 10.30 - 11.30 am and by appointment
    • Harrison 318
  • Ph.D. students and postdocs are welcome.


Approximate Course Content

The following may change as the course progresses.

  1. Equations in a rotating frame. Boussinesq and anelastic equations. Conservation properties.
  2. Hydrostatic and geostrophic balance. Static stability.
  3. Shallow water equations. Surface waves. Potential vorticity. Geostrophic adjustment.
  4. Geostrophic Theory, Quasi-geostrophic and planetary geostrophic equations,
  5. An introduction to waves. Group velocity. Rossby waves.
  6. Generation of jets and mean flows. General circulation of the atmosphere
  7. Ekman layers. Simple models of ocean gyres.
  • In addition, from time to time we may explore various concepts numerically as well as analytically, mainly using simple Python codes.
