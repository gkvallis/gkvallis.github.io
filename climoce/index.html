Climate and the Oceans

by G. K. Vallis

This is a short (240 pages, about the size of a paperback novel) book on climate and the oceans, recently published by Princeton University Press (PUP). It is part of a series called 'Princeton Primers on Climate'. The books are written at the undergraduate level, and might also be used by graduate students or scientists in other fields as a quick read to get the big picture, or by the educated and motivated general reader. The figures are also available for free download for use in the classroom.
