\begin{narrower}We propose the establishment of Center in climate prediction and
assessment, concentrating on time-scales longer than the
seasonal-to-interannual. The Center will involve both GFDL and Princeton
University.  Our ultimate goal is to have a predictive capability for
the climate system on timescales from the seasonal-to-interannual to the
centennial timescales on which the signal from anthropogenic climate
change dominates. The activities of the Center will be designed to
complement and supplement existing research at GFDL and Princeton
University in such a way as to create a pre-eminent center for
climate prediction, fully integrated with NOAA's Climate Services.

The activities of the Center will include the continual production of
ensembles of climate integrations for the next 50 years, incorporating
the present state of the climate system as initial conditions. This
will involve assimilation of ocean data (for example data from the
Argo float program) into our models. The integrations will be
accompanied by assessments of the confidence we can place in them, and
thereby assessments of the predictability of climate on decadal
timescales. The data and reports will continually be made available to
the public and the scientific community.  The accompanying scientific
activities will include investigations into the mechanisms of climate
variability on decadal and multi-decadal timescales, including the
variability that has occurred in the past, and the interaction of
natural variability with anthropogenic climate change.
