Advection-Diffusion-Condensation

Here is a simple advection-diffusion-condensation model, as described in Chapter 18 of AOFD. In this code a single clockwise streamfunction advects water vapour. The water vapour souce is the bottom surface, which is saturated. The temperature falls linearly from 26 C at the ground to - 50C at the top. The saturated mixing ratio is calculated using a Clausius-Clapeyron relation. The water vpour condenses when it becomes saturated, in a 'fast condensation' limit. The advection is a simple, flux-form, scheme. In this code the streamfunction is assumed constant
