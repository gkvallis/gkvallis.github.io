Fluid Dynamics of the Atmosphere and Ocean

Introduction

This is an informal web site for material related to the module Fluid Dynamics of the Atmosphere and Ocean (ECMM719). It should be used in conjunction with the ELE site, which is available to University of Exeter students.

  • Credit value: 15
  • Pre-requisites: Undergraduate maths, physics or engineering, including vector calculus and fluid dynamics. Ideally classes equivalent to ECM2706 and ECM3707. Or permission of the instructor.
  • Assessment:
    • Written exam (closed book) 80%
    • Course work. 20%. 2 assessed problem sets, various unassessed sets.
  • Lectures times:
    • Tuesday 9am, Wednesday 11am, Thursday 1pm
    • Harrison 254
  • Office Hours:
    • Tuesday 10-11am, Wednesday 12-1pm, or by appointment
    • Harrison 318
  • Ph.D. students and postdocs are welcome.


Approximate Course Content

The following will likely change as the course progresses.

  1. Equations in a rotating frame. Boussinesq and anelastic equations. Conservation properties.
  2. Hydrostatic and geostrophic balance. Static stability.
  3. Shallow water equations. Surface waves. Potential vorticity. Geostrophic adjustment.
  4. Vorticity, potential vorticity, circulation theorem.
  5. Approximate sets of equations of motion. Quasi-geostrophic and planetary geostrophic equations.
  6. An introduction to waves. Group velocity. Rossby waves. Gravity waves
  7. Wave-mean flow interaction. Wave activity. Generation of mean flows.
  8. General circulation of the atmosphere. Maintenance of mean winds.
  9. Introduction to Ekman layers
