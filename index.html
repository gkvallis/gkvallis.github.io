About me

I am a professor in the Department of Mathematics at the University of Exeter. Links to my papers, books, present and past students and postdocs, job openings and so forth are to be found either in the left column or at the top of the page. Please email me if you don't find what you are looking for.

I am interested in the following general areas.


  • Geophysical Fluid Dynamics
  • Climate Dynamics
  • Circulation of Planetary Atmospheres (including Earth!)
  • Dynamical Oceanography
  • Turbulence, especially geostrophic turbulence
My new book Essentials of Atmospheric and Oceanic Dynamics is now published and available. To find out more, click here.
